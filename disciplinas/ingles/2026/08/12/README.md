## Writing a Professional Bio

A professional bio is a short introduction that tells people who you are, what you study, what you do, and what you want to achieve. It is usually written in the third person or in the first person, depending on the context, and it should be clear, simple, and confident.

Example:

"Hi, I'm Lucas, a student at Fatec Rio Claro studying software development. I am interested in technology, programming, and solving real-world problems with practical solutions. I am looking to improve my skills, work with other people, and build a strong career in the tech field."
